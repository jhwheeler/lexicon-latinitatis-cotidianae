# Baking: zest, crust, filling, and the rest (23 November 2026)

Jack, 23 November 2026, making a lemon pie: "there is no word for zest maybe also other words are missing". 111 baking phrases were checked against the lexicon; 44 were answered. "Zest" led only to *eager*; crust, a pie's filling, yeast, meringue, and most of what one says while baking had nothing. Two Opus researchers took the 70 gaps (report only); Claude (Fable) vetted and applied.

20 records are new and 42 gained wordings, examples, or terms. All are drafts.

## New records

zest, filling (of a pie), pastry case (and the base of a cake or tart), crust, tart, meringue, custard, icing, sprinkles, condensed milk, starch (with cornflour), baking powder, baking tray, baking paper, cooling rack, prick, blind bake, set (go firm), sink (of a cake), sweeten.

Yeast is under leaven (*fermentum*, which Lewis & Short glosses "leaven, yeast"); grater under cheese grater (*rādula*, with *scobīna*); lemon pie, lemon meringue pie, cake tin, brown and caster sugar, "it has risen", "golden brown", "turn it out", "a slice of pie", and the like are wordings and examples on the records that had the word.

## Household words

Three answers are the words the household already uses, and they lead as ours:

- zest: *cortex rāsus* (of a lemon, *cortex citreī rāsus*)
- baking powder: *pulvis fermentārius*, with *nitrum* for baking soda and Morgan–Owens's *soda pistōria* beside it. The researcher's *pulvis pistōrius* was not needed and was left out.
- cooling rack: *crāticula* (classically a gridiron)

## Other terms that are ours

meringue: *spūma ōvōrum*; baking paper: *charta pistōria* (Cato did the same work with oiled bay leaves, Agr. 76, 121, which stand as examples); blind bake: *solum ināne prius coquere*; sprinkles: *grāna saccharī versicolōria*; icing: *crusta saccharī*. We have found no word for any of these in the authors, Traupman, Morgan–Owens, or Levi–Bini.

## From the authors

- filling: *impēnsa* (Apicius 4.2.14, *tot trullās impēnsae*), with *fartum*
- pastry case and base: *solum* (Cato Agr. 76, *inde facitō solum tenue*)
- crust: *crusta*; a sheet of pastry: *tractum* (Cato)
- tart: *scriblīta* (Cato Agr. 78; Plautus; Petronius)
- starch: *amylum* (Cato Agr. 87; Apicius)
- set: *concrēscere* (Columella, of cheese), *coīre* (Varro); sink: *subsīdere*
- twenty minutes in the oven is *triēns hōrae*, by the twelfths

## Calls for Jack to judge

- **Tart and muffin.** *scriblīta* now leads tart, where Cato, Plautus, and Petronius have it of a cheese tart. The muffin record leads with the same word on Traupman 72. One of them should give way; the muffin record was not changed.
- **Custard.** *patina ex lacte* is Apicius's heading for a savoury dish set with milk and eggs; for a sweet custard it is a stretch. Morgan–Owens has only a Greek coinage (*oeogala*).
- **Condensed milk.** *lac spissātum* rests on Pliny's *omne lac igne spissātur*, as Lewis & Short quotes it.
- **Baking tray.** *lāmina*, a plate of metal, used of a modern thing.
- **Half.** Varro, as Gellius reports him (3.14), calls *dīmidium librum* bad Latin for *dīmidiātum librum* or *dīmidium librī*. The record's own example *Dīmidium pirum mihi dā* now reads *Dīmidium pirī*, and *dīmidiātus* is added.

## Dropped or changed in vetting

- Pliny NH 18.104 on leaven: the book is not in the texts, and the quotation was not Lewis & Short's.
- A separate record for the base of a cake was folded into pastry case: both are Cato's *solum*.
- "Lemon tart" answers on tart, not on pie.
- Macrons: *rāmentīs*, *acūleīs urtīcae*.

## Left open

- cheese grater is really the grater; the record might be renamed.
- berry has "pip" among its wordings, though pips are under seed.
- whip's *peragitāre* and weigh's terms have no principal parts.
