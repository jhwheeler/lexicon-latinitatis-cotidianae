# Coinages only where needed: the pass of 13 November 2026

The editor's principle (13 November 2026): a word or phrase we came up with (a term whose `period` is `ours`) belongs in the lexicon only when it is needed: when no author of any period has a word for the concept, or a circumlocution has a really good reason. Otherwise the word is taken from earlier authors, who need not be classical, though the closer to Cicero the more weight; the modern reference works count as sources (Traupman, Morgan–Owens, Levi–Bini, Andresian). The rule is written up in `docs/records.md` ("Coinages only where needed").

## Scope

Before the pass 416 terms were marked ours. This pass took the 153 that stood in a record beside a term of another period whose status is not `avoid` (127 records). The other 263 are in records with no attested term at all and were left alone, for a later pass.

Each term was read in its record and checked with `scripts/lookup.py`: the English in Traupman, Morgan–Owens (the copy in the latinitas `refs/`, saved 4 November 2026), Andresian and, where it bore on the decision, Levi–Bini; the Latin in Lewis & Short. No claim below rests on anything else.

After the pass 321 terms are marked ours: the 263 out of scope, and 58 beside an attested term (22 needed, 7 household, 29 doubtful).

The eight records that recommended a coinage beside an attested recommended term (balance, blackberry, debug, dice, get off, hold on, transfer, truck) no longer do, and the builder now refuses such a record.

## For the editor: the doubtful terms

Not changed. Each line gives our word, the attested word beside it with its source, and a recommendation.

- **backpack**: *pēra dorsuālis* vs *mantica* (Horace; Traupman 275), *saccus dorsuālis* (Morgan–Owens) and *saccipērium dorsuāle* (Traupman 336). Recommendation: *pēra* is the household word for a backpack, so keep it; for other readers *mantica* could be the recommended word.
- **basement**: *cella subterrānea* (its comment is only "For Theo") vs *hypogēum* (Vitruvius; Traupman 122; Morgan–Owens) and *cellārium subterrāneum* (Traupman 122, 288). Recommendation: if it is a family word, keep it and say so; if not, move it to the examples.
- **deodorant**: *odōrāmentum* (classically a perfume) vs *odōrem auferēns* (Levi–Bini p. 21). Recommendation: keep ours: Levi–Bini's is a description, not a noun.
- **educational**: *ad discendum ūtilis* vs *didascalicus* (Traupman 52, in *pellicula didascalica*; rare in antiquity). Recommendation: recommend Traupman's word and keep ours as an example.
- **environmental**: *nātūrae* (the plain genitive) vs *oecologicus* (Morgan–Owens, after Egger). Recommendation: keep ours: plain Latin with a stated reason; it is hardly a coinage.
- **episode**: *āctus* vs *episodium tēlevīsificum* (Andresian p. 15, not in Lewis & Short). Recommendation: keep ours.
- **folder**: *scrīnium* vs *cooperculum* (Traupman 261, 317; classically a lid). Recommendation: keep ours.
- **fruit stall**: *mēnsa pōmāria* vs *taberna pōmāria* (Morgan–Owens s.v. fruit stand). Recommendation: keep ours: Lewis & Short gives *mēnsa* as a market stand (*mēnsa laniōnia*, a butcher's stall, Suetonius). The vegetable stall, *mēnsa holitōria*, goes with it.
- **headphones**: *auscultābula* (household usage; Traupman's *auscultābulum* is a telephone receiver) vs *conchae acūsticae* (Morgan–Owens; Andresian p. 15), which are the large kind only. Recommendation: keep ours: no reference has a word for earphones.
- **lollipop**: *dulciolum bacillō praefīxum* vs *līchidium* (Andresian p. 10, no other source). Recommendation: keep ours, but its comment says no source has a word, which Andresian contradicts: correct the comment.
- **mailing list**: *catalogus dēstinātāriōrum* vs *grex* (Traupman 261, 340). Recommendation: recommend Traupman's *grex* and keep ours as an example for a list of recipients.
- **metric**: *index numericus* and *index* vs *mēnsūra* and *numerus* (classical). Recommendation: recommend *mēnsūra* and drop both of ours: *numericus* is not in Lewis & Short.
- **pause (a recording)**: *intermittō* vs *suspendō* (Andresian p. 15; classically to hang up). Recommendation: keep ours.
- **recycle**: *recoquō* vs *regignō* (Andresian pp. 2, 11; once in Lucretius, "beget again"). Recommendation: keep ours.
- **restart**: *dēnuō excitāre* vs *redinitiāre* (Traupman 262, 369). Recommendation: recommend Traupman's word and keep ours as an example.
- **ringtone**: *sonus*, and *tinnītus* alone, vs *tinnītus tēlephōnicus* (Traupman 391). Recommendation: recommend Traupman's phrase; *sonus* as an example.
- **rolling pin**: *cylindrus* (household usage) vs *fistula* (Traupman 124, 372; classically a pipe). Recommendation: keep ours: Cato and Vergil use *cylindrus* of a roller (Lewis & Short).
- **scan**: *trānsferō* vs *scānsōrium*, a scanner (Traupman 262; Andresian p. 14): a noun only. Recommendation: keep ours: no reference has the verb.
- **slide**: *tabula dēclīvis* (household usage, an alternative) vs *tractus lūbricus* (Morgan–Owens). Recommendation: keep it as the household alternative.
- **spreadsheet**: *ratiōnārium* vs *charta computātīva* (Traupman 262, 384). Recommendation: recommend Traupman's phrase, with *ratiōnārium* as the alternative; either way the comment "no reference work has a word" is wrong.
- **toy bear**: *ursulus lūsōrius*, marked ours here, though the teddy bear record cites the same phrase from Morgan–Owens (not in our copy of Morgan–Owens). Recommendation: merge the record into teddy bear, and check that citation.
- **trailer**: *carrus quī trahitur* vs *currus remulcātus* (Morgan–Owens s.v. trailer, caravan, RV, after Egger). Recommendation: take Morgan–Owens's phrase if it covers a cargo trailer; ours to the examples.
- **unemployment**: *quaestū carēre* vs *operā carēns* and *invīta operis vacātiō* (Morgan–Owens, the second after Egger). Recommendation: recommend Morgan–Owens's *operā carēns* and keep ours as an example.
- **variable (in programming)**: *variābilis* vs *quantitās variābilis* (Euler, a citation the record itself marks as unchecked). Recommendation: check Euler; then the full term is recommended and the short form its alternative.
- **video**: *pellicula* vs *segmentum cinēsiopticum* (Morgan–Owens, with a query). Recommendation: keep ours: Andresian (p. 15) has *pellicula* for a film, so it could be relabelled modern.
- **visa**: *ingressūs diplōma* vs *ingrediendī licentia* (Morgan–Owens, with a query). Recommendation: recommend Morgan–Owens's phrase, which is plain Latin; ours as the alternative.
- **yogurt**: *lac concrētum* vs *jogurtum* and *oxygala* (Morgan–Owens). Recommendation: probably Tacitus's phrase (Germania 23), which could not be confirmed in the references at hand: check it, then relabel it classical or delete it.

Three things done that the editor may want to reverse or take further:

- **blackberry**: *mōrum rubī* is not ours: Morgan–Owens (s.v. berry: blackberry) gives it from Mattioli (1571). It was not deleted; it is now an alternative labelled Renaissance, and *mōrum* alone is recommended. Say if it should go all the same.
- **pyjamas**: *vestis nocturna* was not touched. Morgan–Owens s.v. pyjama has *vestis dormītōria (v. nocturna)*, after Helfer, so the household word has a source and could be labelled modern, not ours.
- **bug** and **debug**: *mendum* and *ēmendāre* are classical words used in their own sense (a slip in writing; to correct), marked ours only because the thing is software. They were kept as needed; relabelling them classical would be truer.

## Noticed on the way, outside this pass

- Some records cite Morgan–Owens for words that are not in the copy of the *Adumbratio* kept in the latinitas `refs/` (teddy bear, *ursulus lūsōrius*; truck, *vehiculum onerārium*; excavator, *māchina fossōria*; slide, *tractus lūbricus*; horizontal bar, *ferrum trānsversum*). They may come from the newer lexicon at neolatinlexicon.org; they were left as they stand and not relied on.
- Several terms labelled modern carry no evidence (*exitus rēālis* under actual result, *programmātiō* and *programmātūra* under programming, *ursulus* under toy bear, *ē birotā dēscendere* under get off). They count as attested for the builder's rule; they should be given a source or relabelled.
- *my turn* (*Nunc ego*) and *vegetable stall* (*mēnsa holitōria*) are among the 263 and mirror *your turn* and *fruit stall*, which this pass changed or put to the editor.
- Morgan–Owens has further attested words that records could take: *resultus*, a bounce (Vives); *vaccīnium palustre*, cranberry; *plaustrum (automatum)*, truck; *cēnāculum*, a furnished attic.
- In several records the Traupman line under Sources lacks pages cited in the terms (bark, jug, meow, soup, truck) or has no pages at all; this was so before the pass.
