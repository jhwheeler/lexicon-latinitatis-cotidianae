# Macron slips found by the linker (15 November 2026)

The builder links each Latin word to the entry of the word it is a form of, and compares forms with their macrons. `notes/coverage/unlinked.md` listed 538 words in 294 rows that would belong to an entry if their macrons were other than they are. Jack’s judgment (15 November 2026): macron slips are a big deal and must be fixed. Every row was read against Lewis & Short (`scripts/lookup.py ls`), the house rules of the Latinitas `AGENTS.md` section 2, the rulings, the exceptions file and the notes of the records; where Lewis & Short leaves a hidden quantity unmarked and the lexicon had no note, the page of de Vaan (2008) was read as an image, because the searchable text of de Vaan has lost its macrons. 303 words were corrected in 221 records: 239 in texts (examples, quotations in evidence, comments, heads of several words) and 64 in thirteen dictionary forms and the texts that copied them. One line was added to `data/quantity-exceptions.json` (*impūne*). Nothing but macrons was changed, and no record’s `id` or file name.

| | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “Found only when the macrons are ignored” | 538 words, 294 rows | 301 words, 151 rows |
| The builder’s totals | linked 102,679 (84.7%), ambiguous 10,583, without an entry 6,553, names 1,469 | linked 102,890 (84.8%), ambiguous 10,610, without an entry 6,315, names 1,469 |

Of the 294 rows, 135 were slips in texts, 8 were slips in entries, 139 were neither (three of these also held a slip that was corrected: *ā*, *eī*, *nesciō*), and 12 are left for the editor. The 301 words that remain are 275 the linker cannot know and 26 that wait for the editor.

Three of the examples given with the task turned out otherwise than a quick look suggested. *bīduum* is right and the entry *biduum* was the slip: Lewis & Short heads the adjective *bidŭus* but prints the substantive *bīdŭum* in the same entry, and the record within-a-time already had a note for the long ī. Lewis & Short has *cĭtŏ*, not *citō*, so the entry *cito* stands and twelve *citō* in the texts were corrected (the linker had not listed them, since it took them for the verb *citō*). *stāgnum* and *ānsa* were already right in the entries; the texts were wrong.
