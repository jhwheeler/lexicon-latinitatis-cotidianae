The public [PDF](https://paulatinygriego.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/conversational-latin-for-oral-proficiency-traupman.pdf)
was consulted during the vocabulary lookup. Printed pages used here:

- 53, 389: **oscillāre** explicitly intransitive; **oscillum** the swing.
- 116, 286: cap **pilleus**.
- 122, 280: blanket **lōdīx**.
- 123: clothes hook **uncus vestiārius**.
- 124 (also 360, 404): water pipe **fistula aquāria**.
- 293: collision **collīsiō**.
- 389: wipe off the table **mēnsam pertergere**.

The PDF's extracted text substitutes characters for some accented vowels.
Latin quantities are cross-checked against the dictionary forms and existing
lexicon conventions rather than copied from those substitutions.

### Morgan–Owens

Direct headwords checked:

- [Rod](https://neolatinlexicon.org/latin/rod/): distinguishes fishing and surveying rods; lists **virga**.
- [Pipe (plumbing)](https://neolatinlexicon.org/latin/pipe_plumbing/): **fistula aquaria**, **tubulus**.
- [Gravel](https://neolatinlexicon.org/latin/gravel/): includes **glarea**; the medical **calculus** is a different sense.
- [Blanket](https://neolatinlexicon.org/latin/bedcover__blanket/): **lodix**, among alternatives.
- [Playground swing](https://neolatinlexicon.org/latin/park__swing_in_playground/): **oscillum**, citing Albert.
- [Move, noun](https://neolatinlexicon.org/latin/move_a_playing_piece_checker_noun/): **mōtus / tractus** in the game sense.
- [Somersault](https://neolatinlexicon.org/latin/somersault/), [verb](https://neolatinlexicon.org/latin/somersault_vi/), [somersaults/cartwheels](https://neolatinlexicon.org/latin/somersaults__do_somersaults_turn_cartwheels/): distinguish **saltūs cernuī**, **cernuare**, and a cartwheel description. The singular **saltus cernuus** is supplied by inflection; no precise modern gymnastics classification is asserted.
- [Military trench](https://neolatinlexicon.org/latin/trench_as_those_of_wwi/): qualified **fossa castrensis**. Plain **fossa** suits the house trench.
- [Lid / container cap](https://neolatinlexicon.org/latin/lid_cap_of_container/): **operculum**.
- [Hat: cap](https://neolatinlexicon.org/latin/hat__cap/): **pilleus**, among alternatives.
- [Collision (auto)](https://neolatinlexicon.org/latin/collision_auto/): **collisio** citing CL; **collisus** citing LRL.

### Ancient dictionaries, Vatican and Vicipaedia

Lewis & Short supplies the relevant ancient meanings and forms. Direct entries
linked in the cards cover *uncus, hamus, affigo, libro, oscillo, impello,
fistula, tubulus, glarea, motus, inverto, fossa, fodio, terra, sordes,
tumulus, acervus, cumulo, congero, tergeo, comprimo, exprimo, contundo,
contero, collido, fragor, munus, mandatum, expeditio*, and others.

- **lībrāre**: II.B–C includes suspended swaying and transitive swinging/brandishing. It is not treated as the universal translation of swinging any object or person.
- **oscillāre**: dictionary cites Festus for swinging oneself. The ancient verb is not relabelled a modern invention.
- **contundere**: perfect quantity varies in the cited evidence; **contudī** deliberately has no added root-vowel macron. The supine **contūsum** follows the marked dictionary form.
- **pilleus** follows Traupman's spelling; the alternative **pīleus** is distinguished rather than combining its vowel quantity with the double-l spelling.
- **virga ferrea**, the hook-verb phrases, the swing-pushing prompt and all family examples are our compositions from recorded words. They are not separately attested technical headwords.
- The Vatican's public [*Parvum verborum novatorum lexicon*](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/institutions_connected/latinitas/documents/rc_latinitas_20040601_lexicon_it.html) was checked in the lookup; it furnished no better match for these physical senses. Its online selection is not the complete *Lexicon recentis Latinitatis*. The **collīsus** LRL attribution above is specifically Morgan–Owens's citation, not a claim to have inspected the full Vatican volume.
- [Vicipaedia: Lodix](https://la.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lodix) supports contemporary blanket usage and supplies ancient dictionary references. It is corroboration, not the sole authority for quantities.
- Quest/mission choices are explicitly contextual: ancient *expeditio* is primarily a campaign/expedition; a playful quest is our application. *Investigatio* names searching, while *munus* and *mandatum* name an assigned duty or commission. Card 96's established *ausum* preference for adventure remains.
