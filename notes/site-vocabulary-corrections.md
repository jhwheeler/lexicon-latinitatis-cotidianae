# Site and construction vocabulary: classical sources and corrections — 26 October 2026

Prepared against current `main` commit `da9364bcb61a476709c96f44249bd1147c7039c5`
(PR #3 merged). This batch corrects 27 existing cards, adds 2 cards (190–191),
and brings the total to 191. English–Latin, with Bulgarian glosses where the
construction and house-layout cards already carry them.

It applies the lexicon review in the *Domus nova* research for Episode VII.
That review checked Lewis & Short in the local Perseus XML, and the Latin texts
locally or in the CLTK Latin Library mirror. Each locus added here was
re-read in those texts, and each quantity written was compared with Lewis &
Short, before the edit. Section numbers follow the modern editions. Where
Lewis & Short uses older numbers, the card says so.

## Decisions

- **norma** is written without a macron. Lewis & Short leaves the vowel
  unmarked, and the reader series writes *norma*. Traupman's *nōrma* is no
  longer followed (card 143).
- **tabula spūmea** is our working formation for EPS insulation board. It is
  built on classical *spūmeus* "foamy" and is not attested. The boards on site
  are black graphite EPS, so the gloss does not call them white (card 152).
- **Scaffolding** leads with **māchina**, the Roman word, with the member names
  **arrēctāria** and **trānsversāria** from Vitruvius. *Compāgēs* is demoted to
  modern lexicographic use (Morgan–Owens) (cards 172–173).
- The hooked-stick example now reads **Theodōrus bacillum aduncum ex
  trānsversāriō māchinae suspendit.** It replaces *ex vecte suspendit*, since a
  scaffold tube is not a *vectis* (cards 159, 173).

## Quantities and forms checked

Every headword touched or added was checked in Lewis & Short. Points of note:

- **coctilis** (short i), **norma** (unmarked), **hypogēum** (ē; also *hypogaeum*),
  **harēnārium** (*hărēnārĭum*), **sōlārium**, **diaeta** (short i), **calcārius**,
  **fornāx, fornācis**, **trānsversārius**, **pertica**, **pluteus**, **lōrīca**,
  **retinācula**, **cumulus**, **congestus, -ūs**, **bacillum**, **calyx, calycis**,
  **putāmen, putāminis**, **fistūcō**, **ligō, ligāre**, **dolō, dolāre**,
  **ascia** and **spūmeus** follow Lewis & Short's marking.
- Lewis & Short does not mark hidden quantities in **arrēctārius** (*rēctus*),
  **adstrūxī, adstrūctum** and **exstrūxī, exstrūctum** (*strūctum*),
  **redēmptor** (*ēmptum*), **pīnsō** (*ī* before *ns*) and **īnferciō** (*īn-*
  before *f*). These follow the lexicon's existing hidden-quantity convention
  (Bennett, with later corrections).
- **statūmen** is given without a genitive. Lewis & Short prints "*īnis*", against
  the regular short *-inis*, so neither form is asserted.
- Quotations are macronized to match the headwords. The Vitruvius 6.8.1
  quotation keeps the Latin Library spelling *concamarātiōnēsque*.
