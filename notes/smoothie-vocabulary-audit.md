# Smoothie vocabulary audit

Reviewed 18 October 2026 against *Family Morning Smoothies*, the six-page, 16-recipe family guide. The repository's existing 104 grouped entries were retained; 27 grouped entries were added (105–131). This repository remains authoritative. No Library lexicon snapshot was edited.

Search **smoothie** in the [live lexicon](https://jhwheeler.github.io/lexicon-latinitatis-cotidianae/) to find the complete addition. The entries sit in Nature & Food and Kitchen & Drinks. The IDs below are grouped entries, not counts of individual words.

## Sources actually consulted

- John C. Traupman, *Conversational Latin for Oral Proficiency*, the user's uploaded PDF. Food-and-drink vocabulary: printed pp. 70–73; kitchen knife and refrigerator: p. 124; blender: p. 280; peanut butter: p. 358; spinach: p. 383. This copy's PDF page number is one higher than its printed number. The original p. 280 image was rendered and inspected to verify the blender's spelling and macrons; extracted text alone encodes several marked vowels incorrectly.
- [Morgan–Owens, Adumbratio Lexici Anglico-Latini](https://neolatinlexicon.org/adumbratio-old/): sections 341–342 (produce), 348 (herbs, spices and sesame), 954 (kitchen equipment), 932 (refrigerator), 302 (nutrition), 445 (calcium), 76 (time), and the preliminary botanical vocabulary. These are lexicographic records and proposals; not all expressions there have an independent quotation.
- [Morgan–Owens, Silva](https://neolatinlexicon.org/silva-old/): the freezer entry under kitchen vocabulary and protein under eating vocabulary. References to Helfer, Eichenseer, Heyse, the Lexicon Recentis Latinitatis and Du Cange are reported through Morgan–Owens, not represented as independently inspected original editions.
- Lewis & Short through [Alatius](https://alatius.com/ls/) and [Logeion](https://logeion.uchicago.edu/), particularly the preparation verbs and older utensil words linked in the entries. Readable Alatius entries were obtained for *tero, contero, seco, minuo, exprimo, eluo, decortico, rado, misceo, metior, pendo, calefacio, refrigerare, zingiber, radula, rudicula, manipulus, juglans, butyrum, holus, cortex* and *fibra*. Some headword requests returned server errors; they are not treated as successfully inspected evidence. The readable *refrigerare* page supplies the cooling verb. The distinction between *concīdo* and *concido* was also checked in the [Corriere Latin dictionary](https://dizionari.corriere.it/dizionario_latino/Latino/C/concido_2.shtml).
- [Prescription-abbreviation appendix, reproduced veterinary prescription rules](https://docs.dtkt.ua/doc/z1452-22): the full reproduced appendix explicitly equates **gramma** with gram and **millilitrum** with millilitre. This supplies modern technical usage, not an ancient metric sense or certain quantities for every modern stem vowel. University PDF search excerpts corroborated the forms, but direct access to those PDFs was blocked; they are not cited as inspected pages.

## Attestation and quantity decisions

| Term or issue | Editorial decision |
| --- | --- |
| Blender | Prefer Traupman's **māchina concīsōria**. Morgan–Owens records alternatives. Machine-part descriptions are labelled as our applications. |
| Smoothie | Use contextual **potiō**, with an explicit descriptive phrase when needed. No unverified supposedly standard one-word neologism is asserted. A whole-food smoothie is distinguished from juice. |
| Spinach | The vegetable sense is medieval. Morgan–Owens explicitly questions the stem quantities, so unsupported stem macrons are not supplied. |
| Banana, kiwi, avocado | Use recorded modern forms. Retain Morgan–Owens's **avac-** spelling for avocado. Do not infer stem quantities for kiwi from an unmarked entry. |
| Celery and parsley | Prefer **apium** and **petroselīnum** respectively; record Traupman's celery alternative. Ancient *apium* can have broader reference. |
| Pumpkin and lemon | Label modern species/sense extensions instead of identifying every ancient gourd or citron with the modern recipe ingredient. |
| Cutting board | **Incīsōrium** is recorded for a medieval trencher/carving board; its chopping-board use is an extension. |
| Peeler, measuring jug, apple core, dicing | Give explicit descriptions, labelled as ours. No claim that a blank dictionary headword was verified. |
| Tahini and peanut butter | Recorded modern expressions. Almond/walnut butter expressions are parallel descriptions, explicitly identified as such. |
| Butter, walnut, fibre, cooling verb | Preserve source distinctions: Traupman's *būtyrum* alongside the long-y alternative; nominative *iūglāns* without projecting that ā into its oblique stem; variable first vowel in *fibra*; long ī in the cooling root. |
| Protein | Follow the marked **proteīnum** in Silva, with no extra unsupported stem macrons. |
| Modern measurements | Modern spoon capacities are specified separately; ancient spoon measures are not equated with 15 ml / 5 ml. Metric units are modern technical vocabulary. |
| Electric-appliance actions | Ordinary verbs are applied descriptively. No invented quotation or claim of universal technical usage. |
